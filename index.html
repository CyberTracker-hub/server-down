How it works

      
    
        
  • There are two leaderboards: high scores in Server Survival, and memes made in the Glitch Lab.
    • 
        
  • You need a Solana wallet to post a score, enter a meme or vote. Signing in only signs a text message. It never moves funds and costs nothing.
    • 
        
  • One best score per wallet and difficulty. One vote per wallet per meme. You cannot vote for your own meme.
    • 
        
  • The top 10 on each board are reviewed by hand before any prize is given. Faked scores, vote farming and offensive memes are removed.
    • 
        
  • Prizes, dates and full rules are announced on X before each round. Entering is free.
    •
